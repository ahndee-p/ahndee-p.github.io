{%- comment -%} JSON-LD entity graph. Driven entirely by `entity:` in _data/settings.yml. THE POINT: every page emits the SAME Person node under the SAME @id ({{ site.url }}/#andrew) and then *references* it by @id instead of redescribing it. That's what tells search and AI engines the author of a post, the creator of a project, and the person on the about page are one entity — and which "Andrew Perez" that is. Redescribing the person on each page (the common mistake) creates several weakly-linked entities instead of one strong one. Node types by page: / → ProfilePage (strongest entity signal, on the most-linked page) /about/ → AboutPage _posts → BlogPosting author → Person _works → CreativeWork, or SoftwareSourceCode when front matter has `repo:` other → WebPage Inner pages also get a BreadcrumbList. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign e = site.data.settings.entity -%} {%- assign person_id = site.url | append: '/#andrew' -%} {%- assign site_id = site.url | append: '/#website' -%} {%- assign page_url = page.url | replace: 'index.html', '' | absolute_url -%} {%- assign page_id = page_url | append: '#webpage' -%} {%- if page.url == '/' -%} {%- assign page_type = 'ProfilePage' -%} {%- elsif page.url contains '/about' -%} {%- assign page_type = 'AboutPage' -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign page_type = 'WebPage' -%} {%- endif -%}
